## Diffusion-inspired writing  

Artificial intelligence, writing, meta  

---
![Comparison of Autoregressive LMs and Diffusion LMs by Inception Labs](../assets/gif/comparison.gif)  



When I write I tend to write in an autoregressive manner. 
The software and tools for writing, and even the language itself is oriented around the left-to-right direction 
of generating new characters. This is perfectly normal. But I've been inspired and want to try out a different approach.  

Diffusion language models have recently made some moderate hype waves in the AI research community.
The entire, incomplete work is generated at once and slowly improved. This is a different approach to just creating things in general.  

When I write I have constructed some mental graph and construct each vertex from start to finish.  
But this results in me making incomplete pieces and lose interest, not really getting the message across. 

So here's what I will try to do. For any new content I write here, I will start off with a minimalistic, summary-like revision or 'preview',
where the main point of the piece is communicated clearly and justifications may be "unrendered components" for later evaluation.  

Of course, I cannot literally generate text like a diffusion lm, but the inspiration is based on creating something from start to end that is incomplete throughout,
yet satisfies the most important requirement at first, like how diffusion lms will often generate the rarest or most important tokens first, then construct more functional language around it.
